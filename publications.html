What is a legal commentary?

A legal commentary is an expert-written guide that explains each provision of a law and shows how it applies in practice. It combines in-depth legal analysis with practical insights, helping professionals navigate complex regulations with confidence.

This commentary on the AI Act not only interprets the legal text but also offers concrete examples of how it will impact the development and use of artificial intelligence.

Why is this work special?

The AI Act is the world’s first comprehensive legal framework for artificial intelligence, setting new standards for how AI will be developed and used across Europe.

This book is the first Polish commentary on the AI Act and one of the earliest in Europe. It provides a structured, practical roadmap for lawyers, businesses, and data scientists facing the challenges of this groundbreaking regulation.

What does “in press” mean?

It means the book has already been accepted by the publisher and is now in the final stages of editing and production. Its publication is confirmed and scheduled for Autumn 2025.
